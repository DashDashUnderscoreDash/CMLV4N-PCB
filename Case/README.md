# **CMLV4N 3d Printed Case**

__Items needed:__
- 1x [C3/C5 universal daughterboard](https://cannonkeys.com/products/unified-daughterboard-and-jst-cable)
- 1x [JST cable](https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256809955750969.html) - Ideally very short. I used 6cm, 4 pin, "double reverse" cables. Longer cables can be used, but they may negativley impact the flex
- 4x [SKUF feet](https://keeb.io/products/skuf-silicone-rubber-keyboard-feet)
- 8x [Geon tadpoles](https://saberkeebs.com/products/geon-tadpoles) - The softer the better in my opinion
- 8x [M2 heatsets](https://a.co/d/0gVnzOae) - *M2/H3/D3 in case link dies in the future*
- 8x [wheel weights](https://a.co/d/0inzJoTX) - *0.65"/16.4mm X 0.75"/19mm in case link dies in the future*
- 8x M2x5 screws

__Print settings:__
- I used [Bambu translucent PLA](https://us.store.bambulab.com/products/pla-translucent?id=593999615827689541) and a random white PLA I had laying around
- 25% 3D honeycomb infill
- 3 walls for additional strength
- There is front and back art you can use a for multicolor printing. If you want single color, in your slicing software, you can set them the same color
- Supports needed for SKUF feet areas. *Not required, but highly suggested, to use dedicated support material with 0 Z distance. This will give you the flattest surface area possible. When using no supports the bridging will have less surface area and the SKUF may fall off*

__Notes:__
- The screw holes are a bit small, so getting the screws in may take some force
- Only tested in PLA with Bambu filament and feault profile, on a P1S with a 0.4 hardend steel nozzle
- I figured this would be a travel board so I went a bit crazy with the screw locations. Don't hate me!
